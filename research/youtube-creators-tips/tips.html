How this was collected

The account only serves its ~20 most recent posts to anyone who isn't logged in, so the back catalogue came from the Wayback Machine: every archived permalink under /YouTubeCreators/status/ was harvested for post IDs, then each ID was fetched fresh from X's public per-post endpoint. 18,440 posts by the account were read in full and filtered down to the advice above.
