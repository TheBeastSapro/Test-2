Footage first

Before any shot is generated, Forgecast searches licensed and public-domain footage for one that actually depicts it. A beat served this way costs nothing, and it is a real clip rather than a model's impression of one. Only beats already planned to move are offered — a still is never turned into footage — so this changes what a video costs and never how it looks.
