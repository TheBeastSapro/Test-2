What can I paste?

CSV, tab-separated (a spreadsheet paste works), or JSON. Column names are matched loosely — views/viewCount/plays all work, as do 1.2M and 3 weeks ago. Every row needs a title, a view count and a publish date; a duration lets Shorts be scored separately from long-form. A paste that is nothing but links is read as links, and they go to the two boxes above by kind rather than being read as a failed table.
