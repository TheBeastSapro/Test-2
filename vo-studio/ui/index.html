What makes a good one

        
    
          
  • Your own finished read. A cut of a delivered voiceover beats anything recorded fresh.
    • 
          
  • 8–12 seconds of continuous speech.
    • 
          
  • No music, no long pauses, no clipping.
    • 
        

        

          Expect drift. A zero-shot clone wanders across a long script more than a trained voice does. That is the cost of free generation — the read-check is what catches it.
