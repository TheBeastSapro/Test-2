# The Tiny Test Video

In 1547 the viceroy sent three ships north, and none of them came back.

## The First Voyage

Between 1547 and 1550, the crews mapped the coast. At Angolpo the Japanese lost forty ships.

## The Second Voyage

By 255 BC a returning fleet had reached Syracuse. It was the largest ever built.

## The Third Voyage

Nobody knows what happened next. The records simply stop.
