### 1. Cartoon Cat (~120 words)

Cartoon Cat.

Around three in the morning, a security guard was finishing his rounds beside an abandoned mall when he noticed something standing in a doorway. It was tall and completely black, with white gloves and a wide smile. Then its arm stretched thirty feet across the parking lot without making a sound, and the smile never changed.

**[VISUAL: black cartoon figure grinning around a doorframe of a dead mall]**

Its size changes between sightings, because its body follows cartoon logic instead of physics. One account puts it at thirty feet tall inside a corridor that was never that wide.

To survive Cartoon Cat, stay out of abandoned buildings, especially in the dead hours after three in the morning. Weapons only make it curious. **[JOKE]**

### 2. The Sewer Spider (~120 words)

The Sewer Spider.

On his first week underground, a new city worker joked to his supervisor about the alligators people say live in the sewers. The old man laughed. Then he stopped. It is not alligators, he said. It is spiders.

**[VISUAL: open manhole at night, four long white legs unfolding out of it]**

The only known photo shows four glossy white legs rising out of an open manhole, each one longer than a man, bending at three separate joints, covered in fine spikes. The rest of the body has never been seen, and the original post never describes one spider. It says spiders.

To survive them, stay out of storm drains and away from open manholes. If a cover is off and there is no crew around it, cross the street.

### 3. Siren Head (~130 words)

Siren Head.

A woman visiting an old cemetery noticed a rusted pole on the hill, standing where no telephone pole should be. Then it straightened up. Two air raid sirens turned toward her like a face, and they screeched to life across the graveyard.

**[VISUAL: cemetery at dusk, rusted siren headed giant unbending from a pole shape]**

Siren Head stands about forty feet tall. Plenty of videos invent bigger numbers for it, and one of them insists on thirty feet, but forty is the height its creator actually stated. Instead of a head it has two sirens, and they can broadcast anything, including the voices of people you love.

To survive Siren Head, distrust any familiar voice calling from the woods. Do not count on your car either. It keeps up. **[JOKE]**
