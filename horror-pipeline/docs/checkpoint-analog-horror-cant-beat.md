## 7. Format Decision (title-specific — "Can't Beat")
- **Mode:** B (second-person, editor-friendly), blended with M Simplified escalation. **Section anatomy (6 beats):**
  1. Second-person hook — the ordinary moment it reaches you (~25–40w)
  2. Reveal — "That is [name]." integrated
  3. Confident physical description + honest scale (canon number only, else describe by form) (~30–45w)
  4. How it detects / reaches / traps you — the mechanism (~30–45w)
  5. **THE UNBEATABLE BEAT** (replaces the generic power beat): the canon reason your defenses fail — ran, hid, fought, and why none worked (~30–45w). Every section MUST prove this.
  6. Closer — creepy hammer (default) or one dry line (only on the non-tragic picks) (~15–25w)
- **What each section must prove:** a normal, prepared person still loses.
- **Thin-lore handling:** fold description+mechanism and lean on unknown-as-payoff ("no one who's seen it has explained it") rather than padding.
- **Opener:** The Mimic ("it's already in your house"). **Mid pattern-break:** Deep Root Disease (infection, not a hunter). **Finale promise:** The Iris (can't even reach it). **CTA positions:** mid after #3 (the Alternates peak), outro after #8. Two custom CTAs, woven, unlabeled.
