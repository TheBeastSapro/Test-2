## HARD ERRORS (must fix before publish)

**1. 1:47-1:56 — wrong creature, frozen for 9 seconds.** Under the "Good Boy" title the screen shows a teal/green scaly creature with three bulging eyes and a round lamprey maw, red arrow pointed at an eye. VO at that exact moment: "its face is wrinkled and almost human. Its cloudy eyes have no pupils, and its mouth hangs open without a single tooth or tongue." Canon Good Boy is black-furred, dog-shaped, with a wrinkled near-human face. This is a different creature entirely. It is also the longest static hold in the video. Worst beat in the cut by a distance.

**2. 5:15-6:23 — section title reads `fetid king`, all lowercase, missing "The".** 68 straight seconds. Every other section is Title Case, and the video's own roster card says "The Fetid King" two seconds earlier.

**3. 8:27-9:40 — section title reads `cartoon cat`, all lowercase.** 73 straight seconds. The roster card at 8:26 says "Cartoon Cat" in Title Case, so the video contradicts itself on screen.

**4. 9:17 — Shadow the Hedgehog (Sonic franchise) on screen under the "cartoon cat" title,** with the keyword pop "forgotten mascot". Wrong franchise, wrong creature, and it introduces saturated yellow into a black/white palette.

**5. 3:53 — Seth Meyers late-night clip with burned-in meme caption "NO! THAT'S NOT".** Real named public figure, caption cut off mid-sentence, lands immediately after the mid-roll CTA and kills the dread register.

**6. 4:16 — the Upside-Down Face is upside down the wrong way.** The whole figure has been rotated 180 degrees, so the body hangs inverted and the FACE reads right-side up. Canon is the exact opposite: an ordinary man standing upright with the head attached upside down. Also shown as a full foreground monster; canon says he belongs in the BACKGROUND of photographs.

**7. Bridge Worm is non-canon fan art throughout** (7:35, 7:43, 7:56, and the roster card). The images show a bulbous head with a wide grin of yellow/bloody teeth and red eyes. Canon Bridge Worm has a WHITE false-face flap, two black eyes and a NARROW mouth, with the teeth only revealed when the flap folds back. The narration at 7:29 literally says "two black eyes and a narrow mouth stretched across white skin" while the screen shows the opposite.

**8. 3:25 — real child in the frame.** The Costume Man two-up puts the correct canon night photo on the left and, on the right, a real-world convention snapshot of a blue mascot suit with a small child standing in front of it. Off-brand for a video about a mascot that kills people, and it is an identifiable minor.

**9. 9:43 — outro roster grid has all eight name labels missing.** Eight unlabeled cards, no title. As a recap it conveys nothing.

## STYLE AND BRIEF VIOLATIONS

**10. Full-sentence VO subtitles are used throughout,** which the brief explicitly forbids. Confirmed instances: 0:10 "Then your own voice answers", 0:46 "Then it moves", 1:06 "Running only matters before it reveals itself", 2:12 "Then it waits for you to open it", 3:33 "But the costume did not remain empty", 3:47 "The person in its hand is", 4:52 "but he does more than observe", 6:06 "Nobody knows exactly how the Fetid King", 6:15 "and the person using it disappears soon after", 7:06 "yet it can fold that frame into something almost human", 7:20 "By then it will be running beside you", 9:03 "without bringing the rest of its body closer", 9:39 "The show has already started". These are typed-on line by line, so they double the reading load and compete with the keyword pops.

**Good news:** the actual keyword pops are strong and canon-accurate where they exist. "40ft" (0:13), "Cables/Flesh" (0:23), "radio broadcasts / warning sirens / conversations / calling for help" (0:30-0:34), "Black fur" (1:44), "patience / obedience" (2:36), "Mr. Mascot" (2:59), "only 17 ribs" (5:45), "kingbirth.jpg" (6:20), "pale" (6:40), "20 ft" (7:03), "2 pairs of 9 foot arms" (7:43), "36 human like teeth" (7:55), "rubberhose body" (8:41), "old media / as its doorway" (9:13), "forgotten mascot" (9:16). That is the right instinct. The problem is the sentences mixed in with them.

**11. Palette breaks — green.** 0:13 uses a plain bright-green vertical bar as the height reference next to Siren Head (it communicates nothing, has no label, and is shorter than the creature). 7:56 sets "36" in bright green. House palette is black and white with a red accent only.

- 8:41 "rubberhose body" should be "rubber-hose body"

**13. The three canon dates never appear on screen.** The Upside-Down Face section is built on 1943 (party), 1948 (car crash), 1951 (train disaster). OCR of every second of the video finds no date text anywhere. The vintage photos used are period-correct, which is good, but the strongest keyword pops in the whole script were left on the table. Same for "HAIL KING", which is spoken at 5:34 and never shown.

**14. Roster card font drift.** The 8-card roster (2:41, 3:58, 5:13, 6:25, 7:22, 8:26) uses a bold SERIF face; every section title uses a rounded comic sans. Third typeface in the project. The zoomed roster variants clip labels off the frame edge at 2:41, 3:58, 5:13, 6:25 and 7:22.

**15. Title stroke flips.** Plain black on white-canvas frames, heavy white outline on full-bleed frames, sometimes unstroked on full-bleed (4:00, 4:07). Not a consistent treatment.

**16. Dead white frames** with the title and nothing else: 0:46, 1:21 (title also missing), 2:40 (title missing), 2:56, 3:19, 3:32, 3:46, 3:54 (fully blank), 4:23, 4:52, 6:29-6:31 (three in a row), 7:19, 7:39, 7:52, 8:26, 8:33, 9:02, 9:22, 9:38.

**17. Heavy reliance on AI renders and game models** rather than Henderson's grainy found-photo composites. Siren Head appears in roughly eight mutually inconsistent designs between 0:00 and 1:18. Assets are reused as if new: the hotel hallway dog photo four times (roster, 1:56, 2:13, 2:39), the Costume Man night photo five times, the forest-road plate at 0:01 and 0:27, and the cracked-wall CGI room at 3:27 recurs at 4:41 in a different creature's section.
