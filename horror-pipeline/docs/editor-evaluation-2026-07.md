## Key craft lessons

**Dark ≠ illegible.** Night scenes need 2–3 bright anchors (lit/reflective car, lamp pools, rim light, white-on-dark figures).

## Standing editor-brief items

1. Layered PNG-cutout storytelling as default; full artworks only as 2–4s punches (max 4–8s per asset).
2. Motion on every held image; animate all pop-ins; scene-building = multiple shots, never one long take.
3. Visual change every 2–4s; never >4s on one unchanged frame.
4. Night scenes need readable anchors; dark, not invisible.
5. Comedy-beat gags synced to dry-humor lines.
6. SFX synced to visual cues over dark ambient bed.
7. Canon (owner-relaxed): fan art OK; on-screen anatomy must never contradict the narration line; neck endless, bare-bone skull, no lower jaw.
8. Cartoon/simplified scenes must be night/desaturated.
9. Section intro cards: punch-in zoom into the creature card/roster cell.
