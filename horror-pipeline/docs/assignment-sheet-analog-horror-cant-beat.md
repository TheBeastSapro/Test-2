## Section format for this video (decided here — do not improvise)

- **Title type:** You Can't Beat (inescapability / survival-stakes).
- **Section shape — the 6-beat "Can't Beat" anatomy** (Mode B). Beats may merge/reorder when the section reads better, but beat 5 is mandatory in every section:
  1. **Second-person hook** — the ordinary moment it reaches you (~25–40w).
  2. **Reveal** — "That is [name]." woven into the scene.
  3. **Confident physical description + honest scale** (~30–45w) — use ONLY the size wording in the table; if it says "describe by form," carry scale through the description with no number.
  4. **How it detects / reaches / traps you** — the mechanism (~30–45w).
  5. **THE UNBEATABLE BEAT (mandatory)** — the canon reason normal defenses fail: you ran, you hid, you fought, and here's why none of it worked (~30–45w). Every section must prove a prepared person still loses.
  6. **Closer** — one line (see closer style).
- **What every section must prove:** a normal, prepared person still loses.
- **Thin-lore handling:** for sparse-canon picks, fold description + mechanism together and lean on unknown-as-payoff ("no one who has seen it has explained it") — do NOT pad with invented detail.
- **Word budget:** ~190–210w typical. Let it vary — opener may run ~210–230; the Alternates / Boiled One / Moon can run heavier; keep the leaner ones lean. Do NOT land every section on the same count.
- **Closer style — JOKES DIALED UP (owner choice), with a hard guard:** a dry closing line on most sections is fine and wanted. BUT the tragic / perception entities — **The Alternates, Deep Root Disease, The Boiled One, The Moon** — close on a CREEPY HAMMER, not a joke. Dry closers live on the force picks (The Mimic, Man in the Suit, Godzilla Bloodbath, and the Iris may take a dry or a dread closer). **Never a joke on a death, attack, or transformation beat** — the joke, when used, lives only in the final line and comments on the absurdity, never mocks the victims.
- **Deliberate beat reorder:** #4 Deep Root Disease is the mid-video **pattern-break** — it's an infection already inside you, not an entity that approaches, so open it on the body, not on a sighting.
