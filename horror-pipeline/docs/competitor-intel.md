## Nonagon Diagnosis (the "low views" question)

Upload frequency is part of it, but not the main story. The data:

1. **Cadence:** roughly 1–2 videos/month since Aug 2025, while M Simplified, ndog ceplok, and Prumhy ship 2–4+/month. Slower flywheel, fewer chances at an outlier.
2. **Topic drift is the bigger killer.** Recent off-niche videos flopped hard: Steel Ball Run Stands 21K, Fiction's Dangerous Factions 18K, Strongest Villains 66K, Villains Pt.4 130K (vs Pt.1's 3.4M). Meanwhile his recent ON-niche videos still perform: Backrooms Movie Entities did 88K in 10 days, SCP Dimensions 89K, Minecraft ARGs 652K. The audience didn't leave — he left the niche.
3. **Series fatigue:** Trevor Monsters Pt.1 = 1.9M, Pt.2 = 692K, Pt.3 = 12K. Straight re-runs of the same title decay brutally; each sequel needs a fresh angle, not just "(Part 3)".

**Lesson for us:** ship consistently (2–4/month), stay ruthlessly on-niche, and never sequel a title without a new angle ("Largest" → "Most Aggressive" → "You'd Never Survive" beats Part 2/3/4).

## Standing Rules Added to the Pipeline

1. **Step 0 now checks this watchlist first** — before writing any title, pull the matching/nearest videos from these 8 channels + M Simplified + Ficknime, count creatures, note rosters and angles, log overlap in the rotation log.
2. **Fresh-topic radar:** new analog horror series, new game-horror releases (movie tie-ins, new Doctor Nowhere drops) are outlier opportunities — Nonagon's Backrooms Movie video and ndog's Poppy Playtime videos prove it.
3. **Sequel rule:** never write "(Part N)" without changing the angle word (Largest → Most Aggressive → Zero Chance Surviving → How to Survive).
4. **Title superlative rotation** (from Prumhy/Fickyep/MrUnnerving data): Biggest, Largest, Creepiest, Most Aggressive, Most Disturbing, You CANNOT Survive, That Shouldn't Exist, How to Survive.
5. **Event radar:** check the adjacent pack (Broogli, Chupacabro, MrUnnerving) weekly for waves — movie releases, new series drops, new creator posts. An event-tied title beats an evergreen one while the wave is live.
6. **Topic bench from adjacent data:** Life of Luxury (proven 1.9M ceiling, uncovered by our two style channels), Backrooms Movie entities, Vita Carnis, real-life cryptids.
7. **Niche-bending rule (added 2026-07-19):** import proven FORMATS from outside niches, never topics. Check the donor format has real numbers before transplanting. Durable angles (origins, ranking, timeline) over gimmicks (ShadowForm warning). The Format Import Bench below is the standing menu.
8. **Saturation rule (added 2026-08-03):** a topic is burning out when the *fresh* uploads on it decay while the old flagship still compounds. Judge a lane by its newest 2–3 entries across all channels, never by the month-old winner sitting at the top of the search results.
