## Top 6 things to tell the editor (mistake-preventers)
1. **Source each creature from ITS OWN series** (list below). No generic monster art, no cross-reuse between creatures.
2. **Rolling Giant name-twin trap:** use Kane Pixels' "The Oldest View" Julien Reverchon parade puppet on a wheeled base. NOT the older unrelated "Rolling Giant" creepypasta (the look-away/don't-blink figure). Same name, wrong creature.
3. **Boiled One: no creator name on screen.** No "Doctor Nowhere," "Silas Orion," or channel logo. In-world only (creature + broadcast + "Broadcast-813").
4. **Man in the Suit = 1954 Godzilla rubber suit** (Goji), not modern/CGI Godzilla.
5. **Hold on the creature for every verdict/closer line** (every section ends on an imageless abstract line — beauty shot or title card, never a blank card or random glitch).
6. **On-screen text spelling:** GO OUTSIDE NOW · HIS THRONE · Broadcast-813 · Metaphysical Awareness Disorder / M.A.D. · Crusader 5 · Gemini Home Entertainment. Easy typos: "Metaphysical," "Reverchon."

## Per-creature: look · source · staged scene the script hands the editor
1. **THE MIMIC (Vita Carnis)** — skinless clawed predator, red flesh, long arms; grows human skin when fed. Source: Vita Carnis wiki. Scene: dark apartment, hallway, phone "roommate still at work," footsteps stopping at the bedroom door. Dry closer OK.
2. **THE MAN IN THE SUIT / GOJI (by Unknowingly)** — 1954 Godzilla rubber suit as living flesh. Source: Unknowingly series / explainer stills. Scene: film set, crew cutting the rubber (flesh attached), shotgun blast, head regrows. Dry closer OK.
3. **THE ALTERNATES (The Mandela Catalogue)** — human doubles (white eye-glint) / blank face / pitch-black body with white eyes. Source: Mandela Catalogue wiki. Scene: phone/dispatcher, door with a familiar face, on-screen "your memories" as trust collapses. TRAGIC — no jokes. Mid-CTA comes right after this section.
4. **THE ROLLING GIANT (The Oldest View)** — see trap #2. Source: Kane Pixels "The Oldest View" / Julien Reverchon Giant. Scene: empty mall, puppet blocking a corridor, crashing through storefront glass, escalator gag (stops at bottom, then rides up). Dry humor OK.
5. **THE BOILED ONE** — tall lanky humanoid, raw crimson boiled flesh, melted head, gaping mouth; face filling a static screen. In-world only (see #3). Scene: nature program to static, red face on TV, viewer paralyzed/blinking in bed, smashing the TV. TRAGIC/perception — no jokes.
6. **THE MOON (Local 58)** — oversized too-close moon, faint face + eyeballs; worst footage reads as flesh masses + skeletal figure. Source: Local 58 wiki. Scene: alert flips "stay indoors" → GO OUTSIDE NOW, HIS THRONE across the moon, blacked-out windows, slipping curtain. Night, but keep the moon bright/legible. PERCEPTION — no jokes (owner kept one dry line here by choice).
7. **THE HORNED SERPENT (The Monument Mythos)** — colossal limbs surfacing beneath monuments (never the whole being). Source: Monument Mythos wiki. Scene: Statue of Liberty with a limb under the pedestal, then a US map with limbs under multiple monuments ("one animal, seen through many holes"). Go apocalyptic on the Great Division line.
8. **THE IRIS (Gemini Home Entertainment) — THUMBNAIL** — planet-sized eye, dull orange/red sclera around a black opening, black-hole distortion. Source: Gemini Home wiki. Scene: probe passing where Neptune should be, eye-planet with warped moons, probe pulled through the opening into an organic interior. FINALE + thumbnail — biggest single beauty shot in the video. Dread or dry closer.
