# QC Notes — Doctor Nowhere's Largest Creatures (your draft → final)

Hey — solid draft, this was polish-and-fix, not a rebuild. Below is everything I changed and *why*, so the next one comes in cleaner. The goal isn't to nitpick this script (it's already fixed and shipping); it's so these stop showing up and your drafts start passing with almost no notes. Rules in **bold** are the ones worth memorizing.

## What was already right (keep doing this)

- **Second-person "You're… That is the ___" hooks** throughout — correct format, exactly what the niche rewards right now.
- **No em dashes anywhere.** Genuinely rare and exactly what we want — the script pastes straight into the workflow with zero cleanup. Keep this habit.
- **Length was on target** (~1,700 words, ~9–11 min) with a varied contour, not a flat uniform block.
- **Closers were mostly strong** — dry, short, fear left under the surface ("Amber only needs one clear look inside," "The Doctor doesn't stop once it's chosen who it's watching"). That instinct is right.
- **Name-as-opener + hard cuts** between creatures, no through-line. Correct.

## Fix-once issues on this script

**1. The biggest creature was at slot 2, not the finale.**
Watch Tower (167 ft) opened second; the video ended on the Boiled One. On a title with **"Largest" in it, scale has to escalate and the biggest creature anchors the finale** — it's also the thumbnail promise that keeps people watching to the end. I reordered so Watch Tower closes. Next time, order the roster by retention *before* writing: strong hook first, thin ones buried in the middle, biggest saved for last.

**2. Amber's height fought the canon, and the weight was invented.**
Draft said "13 and 33 feet… weighing around 456 pounds." The fandom doesn't agree on Amber's height (other explainers use ~23 m), and there's no sourced weight at all. **Never invent a number — especially weights, which almost never have a canon source.** When a figure is contested, say so confidently ("estimates vary wildly, from around two stories to nearly six") instead of picking one that a commenter can correct. When there's no number, describe the scale ("towers over the houses it visits") and move on.

**3. The Housewalker contradicted itself.**
It "makes the ground shake" and drags a house… then "appears able to fly or levitate, hovering off the ground." Those can't both be true, and **the editor illustrates the line literally** — a stomping house and a floating house are two different shots. Pick one that matches the name (it's a *walker*). **Every creature's description has to be internally consistent and match its name.**

**4. A creature had an SCP-style designation — always double-check the universe.**
The Boiled One (PHEN-228, cognitohazard) read like an SCP import. It turned out to be legit Doctor Nowhere canon — but **when a creature has a code/designation that smells like another universe, confirm it's actually Silas Orion before including it.** A Trevor Henderson or SCP creature slipped into a Doctor Nowhere video is the one mistake that breaks the whole premise.

## Standing rules to internalize (these repeat across scripts)

**5. Never open two creatures the same way, especially back to back.**
Watch Tower and Driving Down the Highway both opened on "you're driving at night"; Amber and Housewalker both opened on "You hear"; three sections in a row started with "You're…". **Rotate the opener — setting, character, and the first wrong detail.** If two creatures naturally want the same setup (both road creatures), re-cast one (I moved Watch Tower to "standing at the edge of town at dusk").

**6. Vary the size-beat phrasing.**
Four sections introduced size with "Fan measurements / Fan estimates / Fan sketches place it…". Same template repeated reads as filler. Mix it: "By most accounts…", "People who've lined it up against the houses put it at…", "Estimates place it around…".

**7. No 2+ sentences without a showable image.**
Two spots stalled the editor: Sunman's "sun symbols carry different meanings across cultures" and The Doctor's "gifts or divine messages, though what those are has never been explained." Abstract ideas with nothing to draw = dead air on screen. **Every 3–4 seconds needs something concrete.** I swapped both for images (the afternoon "going heavy like the sun is leaning on your shoulders"; the Doctor "in the edges of their photographs, standing a little further back in each one").

**8. Confident register — state the unknown, don't apologize for it.**
"What little is known about it stays thin," "Fan estimates can't agree," "Very little about it is confirmed" all sound unsure. The house voice is the authority. Say it with confidence: "No one has ever gotten a clean look at its face, and it seems to like it that way."

**9. One creator name-drop per section, max.**
The Birthday Giant referenced "Doctor Nowhere" twice. Spend the one mention on the strongest piece of canon (here, the "almighty jerk" quote) and phrase everything else in-world.

**10. Keep names spelled one way.**
Sunman vs "Sun Man," Doctor Nowhere vs "Dr. Nowhere," "cognito hazard" vs "cognitohazard" all appeared. Pick one spelling of each and stay consistent.

**11. CTA phrasing is fixed, and don't call it a "countdown."**
The mid-roll said "enjoying the countdown" — it's a list, not a numbered countdown. Use the near-house line: "If you've made it this far, you probably like this kind of thing, so a like and a subscribe genuinely help the channel reach more people. Now, back to it."

**12. On a "Largest" title, every creature has to read big.**
Sunman and The Doctor had no stated size. They stay in (The Doctor's the namesake), but the fix is to **carry the scale through the description** — "a towering, elongated figure that stands well above head height," "an impossibly tall body with limbs that stretch far overhead." If a pick can't be made to read large in any honest way, it doesn't belong in a "Largest" video.

## The one-line version

Lock the roster and the numbers before you write (I'll hand you those). Then: rotate every opener, keep one image every few seconds, invent nothing, stay confident, and make every creature honor the title. Do that and the QC pass shrinks to almost nothing.
