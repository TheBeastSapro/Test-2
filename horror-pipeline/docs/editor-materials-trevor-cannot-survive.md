# EDITOR MATERIALS — Trevor Henderson Monsters You CANNOT Survive (script v4)

Reference images and sources per section, in script order. Every creature's wiki page hosts Trevor Henderson's ORIGINAL images in its gallery — use those as the visual ground truth, not random fan art from Google Images (fan art often gets anatomy, scale, and color wrong, and some popular "Trevor Henderson" images are fan-made creatures he never drew).

**General rule for this niche:** if the image isn't on the creature's wiki page gallery or Henderson's own accounts (@slimyswampghost on Twitter/Tumblr/Instagram), treat it as fan art and don't use it as canon reference.

---

## 1. Cartoon Cat
**Reference hub:** https://trevorhenderson.fandom.com/wiki/Cartoon_Cat (gallery holds all original images)
- KEY IMAGE: the debut photo (Aug 4, 2018) — Cartoon Cat peering around a doorway of the abandoned "dirt mall." This is the exact scene the script's hook dramatizes; match the doorway pose.
- Design notes from canon: pitch-black body, white gloves, red gums, crooked teeth, rubber-hose 1930s proportions. Limbs stretch/bend impossibly — animate with "smear frame" motion (Henderson's own description) rather than smooth movement.
- Scale: variable by canon — safe to draw it mall-doorway-sized in the hook, larger in silhouette shots.
- ⚠️ Don't use: gaming/FNF-style Cartoon Cat models or red-eyed variants — fan derivatives, not canon.

## 2. The Sewer Spider
**Reference hub:** https://trevorhenderson.fandom.com/wiki/Sewer_Spider
- KEY IMAGE: the single original photo (Aug 2018) — four long glossy WHITE legs emerging from an open manhole. That is the entire canon visual; everything below street level is unknown.
- Design notes: legs of varying lengths, three joints each, fine hairs/spikes; abdomen partially visible and bare. Keep the body hidden — the unseen body is the point.
- Scale: legs longer than a person (script phrases it that way); total size is a fan estimate, so avoid full-body reveals.
- ⚠️ Don't invent a face/head — none exists in canon.

## 3. The Man with the Upside-Down Face
**Reference hub:** https://trevorhenderson.fandom.com/wiki/The_Man_With_The_Upside-Down_Face
- KEY IMAGES: Henderson's edited vintage photographs — the 1943 Air Force New Year's party, the 1948 car accident, the 1951 train disaster (all in the wiki gallery; these are the EXACT photos the script's "Imagine..." hook describes). Show these three in sequence, then zoom on the figure.
- Design notes: ordinary man, black suit, white collared shirt, head attached upside down, permanent smile, thin bony fingers. He belongs in the BACKGROUND of photos — never show him as a foreground monster.
- ⚠️ The hook is framed as hypothetical ("Imagine scanning..."); visually it can be an album/scanner scene, but the photos inside it should reference the real canon images.

## 4. The Highway Worm
**Reference hub:** https://trevorhenderson.fandom.com/wiki/The_Highway_Worm
- KEY IMAGE: the single original image — pale coiled serpent in roadside underbrush at night. Match that composition for the reveal (flashlight beam finding coils + human-like teeth).
- Related canon for context beats: Bridge Worm page https://trevorhenderson.fandom.com/wiki/Bridge_Worm (the "RIP Photographer Tomas Pearcing" captioned image lives there — usable if you visualize the caption line).
- Design notes: pale calloused brown skin, disturbingly human teeth, NO false face (that's Bridge Worm's trait — don't mix them up).
- Scale: ~30 ft is a fan estimate — keep size ambiguous in frame (partial coils, never full length measured against objects).

## 5. Cartoon Dog
**Reference hub:** https://trevorhenderson.fandom.com/wiki/Cartoon_Dog
- KEY IMAGES: only TWO official images exist. First (April 2020): grinning from a doorway in a ruined building, limbs attached in the WRONG places. Second ("For you, anon"): redraw with limbs in correct places. The script's hook uses the first — wrong-limbs version.
- Design notes: black rubbery body, floppy ears, huge white eyes with dilated pupils, human-like teeth with visible gums, white-gloved hands, arms reaching the floor.
- ⚠️ Cartoon Dog has MASSIVE amounts of fan art and game-model versions — stick strictly to the two official images. When in doubt, it's fan art.

## 6. Siren Head
**Reference hub:** https://trevorhenderson.fandom.com/wiki/Siren_Head
- KEY IMAGE for the hook: the FIRST Siren Head image (2018) — it rising in an old cemetery, described in the original caption (the script's cemetery scene retells this exact canon moment, including the broadcast words NINE. EIGHTEEN. ONE. CHILD. SEVENTEEN. REMOVE. VILE. — those can go on screen as broadcast text).
- Design notes: ~40 ft (creator-stated — the script explicitly corrects bigger invented numbers, so keep on-screen scale consistent with 40 ft ≈ 4-storey building), mummified rust/bark-colored skin, two air-raid sirens facing opposite directions, black wires down the neck, arms nearly body-length.
- Camouflage beats: standing among telephone poles/trees — great for the "pretending to be a pole" line.
- ⚠️ Avoid game-mod versions (green forest Siren Head from the horror game) — proportions differ from canon images.

## 7. Long Horse
**Reference hub:** https://trevorhenderson.fandom.com/wiki/Long_Horse
- KEY IMAGES: debut image (Aug 24, 2018, from Henderson's dream) and the "new sighting. it's on the move" Instagram follow-up (Nov 30, 2018) — both in the gallery.
- Design notes: horse skull WITH NO LOWER JAW (most common fan-art mistake is adding one), endless neck of vertebrae that look like giant human fingers, sparse black hair strands, waxy skin. The neck should trail out of frame — canon says it's infinite, never show an end or a body.
- Tone shift: this section is the twist — gentler music/pacing works; canon says it's friendly and appears to WARN. The dread should come from what its presence means, not from it attacking.
- ⚠️ Never depict it attacking anyone — that would contradict the script's entire twist and canon.
