## STRONG PICKS

### The Mimic — Vita Carnis (Trucidatum Carnis)
Appearance: skinless clawed predator as juvenile; well-fed adult grows real human skin + hair, near-indistinguishable from a person, tells = wide slasher grin, too many teeth, very long fingers; elder ~10 ft, black-skinned but pale face. [Confirmed canon; 10 ft is wiki-stated → treat as Fan-sourced]. Size: describe by form. Behavior: apex predator, mature diet = human flesh; memorizes a victim's routine, disguises with clothing/mannequins, mimics knocking, strikes when you're **alone and asleep**, flees before digesting. **Can't-beat hook:** looks like a person or your pet until too late; learns your schedule; only strikes when you're defenseless; framed near-invulnerable to most weapons. Recognition High · Visual very high.

### The Man in the Suit ("Goji") — by Unknowingly (Godzilla analog)
Grief-broken suit actor whose flesh fused with and filled the rubber 1954 Gojira suit — a living Godzilla; ~10 ft **and still growing**; radioactive/corrosive blood. [Confirmed canon per series + MrUnnerving]. Behavior: massacres crew; **spreads via bites/blood** — other kaiju-suit actors fuse with their costumes → "army of living kaiju"; regeneration, great strength/speed. **Can't-beat hook:** regenerates from decapitation, shrugs point-blank shotgun, tasers only stall — and **fighting him spreads him** (infectious blood turns allies into monsters). Best incident: cameraman blows his head off point-blank; it regrows. Recognition High · Visual very high.

### The Alternates — The Mandela Catalogue (Alex Kister)
Human-replica doppelgängers (minor tells, white eye-glint) OR blank/featureless faces / impossible anatomy; true form pitch-black body, white eyes. [Confirmed]. Size: human-scale. Behavior: patient intelligent hunters that **impersonate your loved ones**; kill psychologically via M.A.D. (Metaphysical Awareness Disorder) — reality-breaking knowledge that drives victims to suicide; **97% mortality (≈100% suicide)**. **Can't-beat hook:** attacks mind + trust, wears the face of someone you love, and canon states **no known way to kill one** (bullets only stagger); sanctioned advice is only to run/hide. Best incident: the "THINK Principle" PSA / the 1992 mandate to destroy all TVs and mirrors (they travel through them). Recognition very high · Visual very high (blank face). **Marquee pick.**

### Gabriel / The Intruder — The Mandela Catalogue
First Alternate, the series' Satan figure; saturated B&W smiling TV-man face (goes pitch-black w/ eyes when enraged); "Stanley" persona = eyes + white-rubber-band mouth. [Confirmed]. Behavior: lives in every screen/mirror, manipulates broadcasts, long-term psychological grooming; 1992 kids' show → **abducted 3,426 children across four counties at once**. **Can't-beat hook:** effectively omnipresent + immortal — inside every TV, screen, and mirror, many places at once, no documented way to harm him; only "defense" was outlawing analog tech + mirrors. Recognition very high · Visual strong. (Same series as Alternates — keep non-adjacent if both used.)

### The Moon — Local 58 (Kris Straub)
Too-close moon with a faint face + eyeballs; in Skywatching the surface reads as organic flesh masses, a spiracle pit, tower-cluster arrow, and a buried fetal skeleton ("HIS THRONE"). [Confirmed]. Size: describe by form. Behavior: **being looked at with the naked eye kills or converts**; hijacks the very warnings meant to protect you ("GO OUTSIDE / LOOK AT THE MOON"); reaches through mirrors and moonlight. **Can't-beat hook:** the act of perceiving it IS the attack; even looking away isn't safe (mirrors, reflected light). **Fresh incident (not the "don't look" broadcast competitors use):** Skywatching "HIS THRONE" / bystander raises hands in worship as screen reads "REJOICE," cameraman gone by the cut to static. Recognition very high · Visual very high.

### The Iris — Gemini Home Entertainment (Remy Abode)
Planet-sized eldritch eye — dull-orange sclera/pupil around a black orifice, spatial distortion, spreading "eye motif" corrupting planets. [Confirmed]. Size: planet-scale (~gas-giant; inconsistent across videos → describe by form). Behavior: sentient malevolent celestial body masterminding a Solar-System invasion; already corrupted Neptune; commands biologically-named moons; seeds the Woodcrawlers/Gardeners. **Can't-beat hook:** you can't out-scale, out-run, or out-fight a planet-sized intelligence already rewriting your solar system; human institutions can at best delay it. Recognition high · Visual extremely high. **Finale + thumbnail pick.**

### Deep Root Disease / Nature's Mockery — Gemini Home Entertainment
Infection: bulb/growth on skin → roots wrap the bones → body becomes a jagged plant-mass rooted to the ground (Nature's Mockery). [Confirmed]. Behavior: progressive parasitic conversion of a human into botanical matter; **victim stays conscious throughout** ("we can hear you"). **Can't-beat hook:** no cure, no reversal — once roots reach bone it's systemic and irreversible, and you're awake the whole time; the only "defense" shown is destroying your own infected before they sprout. Recognition high · Visual very high (body horror). Good **mid-video pattern-break** (not a hunter — an infection already inside you).

## FLAGGED / SWAP CANDIDATES

### The Boiled One — ⚠️ UNIVERSE FLAG (Silas Orion / Doctor Nowhere)
The Boiled One Phenomenon (2024) is credited to the **Doctor Nowhere channel (Silas Orion)**. [Supported]. Tall lanky humanoid, raw crimson boiled flesh, melted head, gaping toothy mouth. Cognitohazard: **perceiving its face** imprints permanent memory + induces "Luant Syndrome" (locked-in paralysis); spreads through hijacked broadcasts. **Can't-beat hook:** you lose the instant you perceive it — danger lives in memory, no distance/weapon/hiding helps, no cure. Recognition high · Visual high. **DECISION NEEDED:** it's a top-tier "can't beat" concept AND genuinely analog horror, but it's a Silas Orion work → including it blurs the Doctor Nowhere lane we reserve for DN-specific titles (slate U11). Recommend flag-and-let-owner-choose; clean swap = Woodcrawler/Skinwalker or Local 58 Fastest-Route creature.

### Woodcrawlers / Skinwalkers / "Fake People" — Gemini Home Entertainment
NOTE: "Mundee" / "The Facing" are **wrong-series names**, not Gemini — corrected. Doorway-filling silent spider-predator; possesses bodies → convincing human-passing "Fake People" (lurching, too-wide grin, mimicked voices). [Confirmed]. **Can't-beat hook:** walls don't hold (burrows up through metal-lined bunkers from below) AND trust fails (wears your family's face/voice). Strong pick — but thematically close to The Mimic (both "looks human, in your house"); use only one of the two as the house-invasion slot, or the Deep Root angle instead. Bench/swap.

### Godzilla Bloodbath — analog cosmic (bench)
Pure-force "can't beat" (nuclear-revived smiling titan, atomic beam). Overlaps competitor "Largest" rosters heavily. Bench as a scale-variety swap.

### Local 58 "Fastest Available Route" creature — (bench, distinct mechanism)
Barely-seen long-armed figure; the horror is the **possessed GPS** that walks you into the kill zone ("your destination is behind you," headlights off). **Can't-beat hook:** your own tools turned against you. Weak creature visual, strong scenario. Good DN-purity swap for the Boiled One. (⚠️ "hunting horns" audio unverified — do not script.)
