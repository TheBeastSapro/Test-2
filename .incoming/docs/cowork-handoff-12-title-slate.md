**Corrected rule:** an earlier hypothesis said the frame needs a *real photograph* to exist. Implication: Analog Horror origins is **viable** (~3.5–4/5), not ruled out as previously stated.

## 4. Standing constraints (from the v5 system doc — enforce these)

- **Canon integrity is non-negotiable.** No invented geolocations, no fake quotes, no fan theory presented as fact. Canon unknowns stay unknown.
- **Sequel rule:** never "(Part N)" without a new angle word.
- **Superlative rotation:** Biggest, Largest, Creepiest, Most Aggressive, Most Disturbing, You CANNOT Survive, That Shouldn't Exist, How to Survive. (DISTURBING now used.)
- **One channel, one niche.** ExplainIt cautionary tale: off-niche uploads did 61–173 views.
- **Cadence:** 2–4 uploads/month. Nonagon's decline was topic drift + slow cadence, not audience loss.

### Verified negatives — do NOT import
- "Explained For Idiots" (MrBread, 595 views — his worst)
- Weakness/utility framing (Nonagon "How To Beat SCPs" 30K vs ndog "How to Survive" 364K). Always survival stakes, never game-guide.
- Quiz/interactive gimmicks (ShadowForm: 1.1M spike → 1.4K in 4 weeks)
- Escalation format "X but It Gets More Dangerous" (Prumhy: 9.6K)
- FNAF × anything right now (Fickyep: 12K)
- Same-title re-runs (M Simplified re-ran "Creepiest SCPs": 23K vs original 605K)
